## In plain words

- **The luck test is now fair.** Before, a rule was compared with a coin that
  flips every bar — it trades so much that costs sink it, so any slow rule looked
  "better than luck". Now each rule is compared with its *own* trades placed at
  random times: same number, same lengths, same costs. A planted "random but lazy"
  rule beat the old coin (RED) and fails the new test (GREEN).
- **US shares are now measured in pounds.** If a US share rises 10% while the
  dollar falls 10%, the test bench now says −1%, as your account would.
- **The safety layer exists and stands in front of every buy.** Every position
  gets a stop that can only go up; each pot has a brake; neither part can spend
  past its slice; nothing is held past 12 months without a fresh decision; the bot
  will not buy into a results day. Any missing figure means no buy. Selling is
  never blocked. The bot is still switched off (S10).
- **The brakes were tested on 22 years of real prices, in pounds.** The 10% brake
  fired in 2008, 2020 and 2022; the 15% one in 2008 and 2020. Ordinary dips also
  trip the 10% brake now and then (one false alarm every two to five years per index).
- **S5 is DONE.**
