## In plain words

- **Your PC is protected first.** Everything QuantBot runs now sits inside one Windows "box"
  (a Job Object), shared by all of its programs:
  - **CPU:** at most 80% of the machine, and only 12 of its 16 processor threads (75%) at any
    instant.
  - **Memory:** at most 80% of the RAM.
  - **Priority:** your own apps always come first.

  It watches the whole PC too: at 80% memory it waits before starting more work, and it stops
  cleanly at 90% for a minute (or 95% at once). This is now a standing rule in your words, and
  only your words can raise or remove it.

  Measured: a program built to grab 100% of the CPU got at most **74.8%** in any second
  (**70.8%** on average) over a minute, and your desktop stayed instant. A program built to eat
  memory was stopped at its limit with a plain message. Details: the table below, and
  docs/reports/QT-23R.md.
- **The advisor exists and is switched on (armed today, 20 October).** It only ever suggests;
  you trade by hand. Its default is "hold the index fund": the whole advisor pot in Vanguard
  FTSE All-World (VWRL), with:
  - a safety stop 15% below its highest close (10% when markets are jumpy) that only moves up;
  - an **URGENT-EXIT** alert if a close falls to the stop. It never sells for you;
  - a buy-back once VWRL closes 10% above its lowest point after an exit.
- **Its first suggestion, today:** BUY VWRL (from Monday's close of £139.02; safety stop about
  £118.17). It is followed *as if* you had done it, on a notional £7,000 pot, at Wednesday's
  opening price, with costs and dividends counted and checked to the penny every day. You can
  read it on the status branch: `status/advisor.md`.
- **The six stock-picking rules from batch 2 are now scored in the background on live prices
  from today.** They can only ever replace the default after 12 months: by beating it after
  costs, passing the luck test, and then still only with your words.
- **One safety check went RED at the very end, and it was my mistake. It is fixed, and nothing
  live was affected.** A wall promises that nothing live can even *reach* the back-test-only
  rule for results dates (your GO of 2026-10-16). The new advisor imported research code that
  reaches it. It never *used* that rule (VWRL is a fund, with no results dates), but the promise
  was broken. I had run only qb2's own tests when committing, not the walls. Three small pure
  pieces moved to modules that don't touch that rule. The wall is GREEN, nothing was weakened,
  and the advisor behaves exactly as before.
- **Two things for you to decide** (details under DECISION REQUESTED):
  1. **The safety stop has historically cost the index fund a lot.** Over 2014–2025, hold-VWRL
     *with* the stop made **+173%**, against **+269%** for simply holding it (both after double
     costs). It sold into four sharp falls (2015, 2020, 2022, 2025) and bought back higher three
     times out of four. It helped in 2020 and hurt in 2022. The stop stays: it is your GO and
     your founding rule ("a moving stop … with every trade"). Only your words can change it.
  2. **After a 10% exit the buy-back can wait forever.** The advisor pot is then cash, about
     10–15% below its peak, and the 10% brake only clears by recovering to within 5% of the
     peak, which cash never does. Your words already clear the 15% hold-off; they do not yet
     clear the 10% brake.
