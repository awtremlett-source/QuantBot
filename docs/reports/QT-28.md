### B2. BELOW-v2's size, fixed by a written rule before running
Rule: X = k × the name's own 1× round trip, k from the fixed ladder {0.7, 0.8, 0.9}; the smallest k whose projected
costs-off luck p < 0.01 on at least one model, projecting by linear scaling of the z-score from QT-27's costs-off
BELOW red-first at k = 0.5 (drill output, not a trial; 200 null runs, so p = (count + 1) / 201).

| k | ridge z → projected p | gbt z → projected p |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 (QT-27, seen) | 1.2844 → 0.0995 (20/201) | 0.9977 → 0.1592 (32/201) |
| 0.7 | 1.7981 → 0.0361 | 1.3968 → 0.0812 |
| 0.8 | 2.0550 → 0.0199 | 1.5964 → 0.0552 |
| 0.9 | 2.3119 → **0.0104** | 1.7959 → 0.0363 |

z = Φ⁻¹(1 − p), z(k) = z(0.5) × k / 0.5, p(k) = 1 − Φ(z(k)). **Even k = 0.9 projects p ≥ 0.01 (ridge 0.0104), so by
the rule k = 0.9 was registered, and the note says so.** Expected, written in the register: costs off → both models
clear Gate 1 (projected 0.010 and 0.036, both under 0.05) → SEEN; honest at real costs → not PASS (each planted trade
earns 0.9× its 1× round trip and pays 1× or 2× of it) → GREEN. A projection, not a promise.
