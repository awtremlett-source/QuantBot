## Part A — the daily front door (4c5bead)

**A1 — the check, on every daily write.** `qb2/data/cross_bar.py`, called by
`qb2/ingest/daily.py` for every file it writes (the after-hours top-up included; a test drives
the after-hours step itself). `front_door.unexplained_jumps` stays as it was (intraday,
report-only); the daily path now has its own check and cannot skip it.

- **Unit break** — a bar whose open is ≥ 3× or ≤ ⅓ of the previous close. The line is cut at
  every break; stretches whose unit agrees (the product of the breaks between them inside
  ⅓..3) count as one unit; **the unit with the most rows is the line's own**, every other
  stretch is quarantined (a tie: the latest). Never multiplied back (SCARS #22).
- **Filler** — rows before the line's first traded bar *on that line* (after unit breaks are
  taken out). **A share's line starts at its first 20 sessions in a row with trades; an ETF's at
  its first trade** (M3: a later no-trade ETF day is a quote). A line with no such start yet keeps
  every row; rates and indices (GBPUSD, ^VIX) have no filler rule.
- **Sticky.** Every quarantined row is kept in `data/clean/quarantine/daily/<name>-crossbar.parquet`
  with its reason and the UTC date found, and stays out of the series on every later write —
  the nightly full re-fetch cannot bring it back, even if the provider "repairs" it (test).
  Lifting one = the operator's words. Starting figures (standing GO): 3×, 20 sessions.
