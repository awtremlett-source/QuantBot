# QT-27 — S6 batch 2, the combined model: registered, built, birth certificate NOT PROVEN, trials not run (Wed 28 Oct 2026)

## In plain words

- **What was built.** Instead of one fixed rule, a small statistical model looks at 17 facts about each share or
  fund an hour after the market opens (how it moved in that hour, overnight, over the last days and weeks, how busy
  it was, how the market and the "fear index" VIX stand) and forecasts how much it will move by mid-afternoon. It
  buys only the best 3 whose forecast is more than twice what the trade costs, and sells before the close. Two
  versions: a simple straight-line model ("ridge") and a tree model ("gradient-boosted trees"), each on three
  groups of names (US, London, both) — six trials, written down and committed **before any code existed**.
- **The birth certificate.** Before a real trial is allowed to run, the model is tried in three made-up worlds
  where we already know the answer. In the world with a strong planted edge it found it every time (4 of 4 PASS).
  In the world with no real information, and in the world with an edge smaller than the costs, it never passed
  either (8 of 8 rejected). **But** two of the exact checks I wrote down this morning were stricter than the
  bench's own rules, and by those words they came out red; and one of the two "break it on purpose" checks did not
  show (switching costs off did not make the too-small edge look good enough to pass the luck test).
- **So the six real trials were NOT run.** Each trial is counted for ever once run, so the box's rule is: if the
  birth certificate is not clean, run nothing counted. Nothing was counted today; **the trial count stays N = 56**.
  No fault was found in the model or the bench — the fault is in how I worded two checks and how small one test
  edge was. Next box: re-write those checks **before** running them, then run the six trials.
- **The cost wall (description only).** In the hours a same-day bot may hold, prices typically move less than
  twice the trading cost for London funds and London shares; about 1 day in 5 rises past it for London funds and US
  shares — and as many fall past it. Over 2014–2025 the world index fund made **all** of its gain overnight
  (+797%); holding only between the open and the close **lost 59%**. A bot that must be flat every night only ever
  stands in the daytime half.
- **The advisor** ran normally last night: £7,009.23 at 1× costs (+0.13%), stop raised to £118.01, all checked by
  hand. One VIX figure was corrected by the provider (it had been read 4 minutes before the official close) — the
  safety door caught and named it, as designed.
- **Nothing is wired to trade; the sender stays disarmed; no decision of yours is needed today.**
