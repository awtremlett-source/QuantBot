## The failing engine test — found, diagnosed, NOT fixed here
`tests/monitors/test_status.py::test_drill_fires_both_meters_and_leaves_no_trace`
fails between 00:00 and 01:00 UK summer time, and it is the clock, not the code:

- the fixture builds its bars and equity marks with `date.today()` — **local**;
- `run_drill` defaults to `datetime.now(timezone.utc).date()` — **UTC**;
- at 00:41 BST local read 2026-09-21 while UTC read 2026-09-20, so the drill
  inserted its doctored low mark at `2026-09-20T23:59:59Z` while the fixture's
  healthy mark sat at `2026-09-21T00:00:00Z`;
- `drawdown_from_peak` takes the LATEST mark, which was therefore the healthy
  one — drawdown read 0%, the light could not fire, and the drill reported
  FAILED.

A **fixture** bug, not a broken monitor: the live drill is unaffected, because
the live book's newest mark (2026-08-06) is far older than any UTC-today insert.
The fix is one line — build the fixture on the UTC date. Left alone because this
box is docs + one test; recorded in `GRAND_TODO.md` under Known defects and in
`STATE.md` under Open flags.
