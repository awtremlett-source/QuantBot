- **`qb2/`** — a skeleton, importable and empty of logic: `data` `research`
  `signals` `model` `sizing` `execution` `ui` `tools`, plus a plain-words
  README. No signals, no model, no orders. All 9 modules import.
- **`docs/plan/ENV_QB2.md`** — generated from `.venv-qb2` rather than typed:
  all 13 pins resolved **exactly** (pandas 3.0.2, yfinance 1.4.1, PySide6

  install command. pandas 3 and the GUI toolkit in one environment is the
  single-environment claim from PLAN_V2 §8, now demonstrated instead of asserted.
- **`tests/qb2/test_env_smoke.py`** — asserts installed versions equal the
  pinned ones, starts Qt offscreen, and checks the skeleton still holds no
  logic. 18 pass on `.venv-qb2`; on v1's `.venv` it FAILS (`pyside6` pinned but
  not installed), which is the point. Its conftest keeps it out of v1's default
  run unless the command line asks for it by name, so v1's daily gate is
  untouched while pointing it at the wrong interpreter is still noisy.
- **The wall, 44 → 52.** qb2 may not import a v1 engine package — and that is a
  real hazard, not a theoretical one, because qb2 has its own `research/`,
  `data/`, `execution/` and `tools/`, so a bare `from research import ...`
  inside qb2 would silently resolve to V1's. Relative imports are allowed. v1
  may not import qb2 (added to the engine's existing ban list, so there is one
  place that answers "what may the engine not import"). The v1 fingerprint must
  not cover qb2, or v2's ordinary work would break v1's proof daily and the
  proof would get abandoned as noise. Planted violation kept at
  `tests/museum/wall_violations/qb2_imports_v1.py.txt`.
- **`.venv-qb2/` in `.gitignore` by repo policy.** It was previously safe only
  because `venv` writes its own `.gitignore` containing `*` inside the folder —
  protection that disappears with the folder. `git check-ignore -v` now names
  `.gitignore:56:.venv-qb2/`, and a wall test asserts the line stays.

# SESSION 2026-09-26 (second sitting) — QT-05: the two-part plan

Docs and one test. No engine code, no qb2 code, S2 not started.

## What changed in the thinking
V2 planned one bot. V3 plans **two parts**, because that is what he asked for: a
BOT that trades by itself on 30% of the account, and an ADVISOR on 70% that
**only ever suggests** — he places every one of those trades himself. Twelve of
his own sentences are pinned verbatim in the plan and in the gate, typos kept.

The single most important line in the whole plan is P1: the advisor does not
trade. The one thing the system does automatically to a holding he bought himself
is move its stop upward, because a stop that needs a human awake is not
protection.

## Built
- `docs/plan/PLAN_V3.md` — 12 verbatim quotes · the two parts · 16 decisions
  P1–P16 each with a named enforcer · carried from V2 · superseded in V2 · 14
  stages S0–S13 · premortem · 3 open decisions · sources.
- `tests/plan/test_plan_v3.py` — written FIRST and run RED (**60 failed, 8
  passed**) before the plan existed, then green at **68 passed**. It pins the
  quotes, requires every decision to name a non-empty enforcer, and pins the
  figures he chose (70/30 and "not shrunk", the 25% primary cap, the 10%/15%
  brakes, the 12-month limit) so round numbers cannot drift.

## The honest limits, written into the plan rather than glossed
We cannot copy Simons: no shorting, no borrowing, and free data a quarter-hour
late. So the plan copies his **method** and says plainly that it does not copy
his speed or expect his returns. Brakes at 10%/15% are labelled *starting
figures, tested first*, and every Trading 212 fact is **UNVERIFIED** until S2
checks it against their own page with a URL and a date.
