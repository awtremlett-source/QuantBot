## A1 — return arithmetic (compounded)

Fault confirmed: stats.compute summed per-bar returns (equity = 1 + cumsum) and
simulate.run measured every held bar against the ENTRY price (a fixed stake);
the benchmark is a compounded holding return. Fix: each bet's slot is re-sized to
the pot every bar (a bar earns its price move on the slot as it stands at that
bar's start; costs come off the slot), the pot's bar return is bet_groups.combine's
equal-weight-per-bet average, equity = cumprod(1 + r) floored at 0, total = equity
end − 1, drawdown on that line; the verdict's benchmark check uses the compounded
total. Sharpe (and the DSR input) stay on per-bar simple returns. STAKE_GBP kept
for the cost arithmetic only (docstring says so).
