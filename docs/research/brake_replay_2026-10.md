## Reading it (written by hand, 2026-10-12)

- **What was replayed.** THE brake (qb2/guard/brakes.step), day by day, on each path
  as if it were a fully invested pot, in pounds, from 2004 (URTH from 2012) to
  12 Oct 2026. The last row of each path is that day's price at 20:06 UK (US still
  open) — it changes nothing above. Closes are price indices: dividends are left
  out, so every recovery reads a little slow, and the false-alarm count leans a
  little LOW (a few dips would have regained the peak sooner with dividends).
- **The crises.** The 10% brake was on in all three windows on every path listed
  then; the 15% level fired in 2008 (all three paths) and 2020 (all four). In 2022 new on
  ^GSPC, ^NDX and URTH in pounds; ^FTSE fell only 13.3% (its "15%" in 2022 is a
  hold-off carried from 2020 that cleared on 4 Jan 2022). As the box expected, the
  weak pound softened 2022's falls for a UK investor: ^GSPC fell 16.8% in pounds.
- **False alarms.** Half to two-thirds of the 10% triggers outside the windows were
  false alarms by the box's rule (7 of 12, 13 of 19, 5 of 9, 4 of 8) — one every
  two to five years per path, ^NDX the jumpiest (13 in 22 years). Most of the rest
  went on to 15% (2010–11, 2015–16, 2018, 2025).
- **What a false alarm costs.** "Return missed" is the path's move from the
  trigger to the day the brake cleared. It is what NEW money would have earned;
  the brake stops new buys, not the holdings already held, so an invested pot does
  not lose it. It is the price of the brake, and it is paid in every episode,
  because the brake clears only after the pot is back within 5% of its peak.

## DECISION REQUESTED

- **10% / 15%: no change recommended.** Both fired where they must, on real
  history, and false alarms come one every two to five years per index. Nothing to decide unless you want a change; P12 stays as written.
- **The bot's 10% brake can never clear by itself** (found while building B2, not
  in the replay): a bot that may not buy holds cash, and cash never climbs back
  within 5% of its peak. The 15% pause already ends with your words (and restarts
  the bot's peak). Recommended: let the same words end the bot's 10% brake too.
  Exact words: **"GO on the bot's brake: my words also clear its 10% brake, and any
  clearance restarts its peak."** Default if you say nothing: as built — the bot
  stays braked until you decide; it is not armed before S10, so nothing waits on it.
